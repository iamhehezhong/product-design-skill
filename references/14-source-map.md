### 经典书籍与理论

- Don Norman, The Design of Everyday Things  
  关键词：discoverability, feedback, affordance, signifier, mapping, constraints, conceptual model.

- Don Norman, Emotional Design  
  关键词：visceral, behavioral, reflective design.

- Fitts, P. M. (1954), The information capacity of the human motor system in controlling the amplitude of movement.

- Hick, W. E. (1952), On the rate of gain of information.

- Hyman, R. (1953), Stimulus information as a determinant of reaction time.

- Miller, G. A. (1956), The magical number seven, plus or minus two.

- Sweller, J. Cognitive Load Theory.

- Kano Model  
  关键词：must-be quality, one-dimensional quality, attractive quality.

- Jobs To Be Done / Clayton Christensen  
  https://www.christenseninstitute.org/theory/jobs-to-be-done/

- Thaler, R. H., & Sunstein, C. R., Nudge: Improving Decisions about Health, Wealth, and Happiness.
  关键词：choice architecture, nudge, defaults, incentives, mapping, feedback, expect error, structure complex choices, commitment devices, social norms.

- Chicago Booth, Think Better with Richard Thaler  
  https://www.chicagobooth.edu/research/roman/stories/think-better-with-richard-thaler

- Chicago Booth Review, Nudge: Preface to the Final Edition  
  https://www.chicagobooth.edu/review/nudge-preface-final-edition

- Cass R. Sunstein, The Ethics of Nudging  
  https://laweconcenter.law.harvard.edu/806_sunstein/

- UK Department for Business and Trade, Evidence review of Online Choice Architecture and consumer and competition harm  
  https://www.gov.uk/government/publications/online-choice-architecture-how-digital-design-can-harm-competition-and-consumers/evidence-review-of-online-choice-architecture-and-consumer-and-competition-harm
